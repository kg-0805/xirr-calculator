Privacy Policy

Information We Collect

This application collects only the minimum information necessary to provide its services:

  • Account information: Email address, full name, and a securely hashed password for authentication purposes.
  • Access requests: Name, email, desired username, and stated purpose — stored locally for admin review.
  • Uploaded files: Excel workbooks are processed in memory only and are never stored, logged, or transmitted to any third party.

How We Use Your Information

  • To authenticate you and manage your login session.
  • To send transactional emails (account creation, password reset codes, reactivation notifications).
  • To process your uploaded workbook and return the XIRR calculation.

Data Storage & Security

  • Passwords are stored as BCrypt hashes — the plain-text password is never stored.
  • Uploaded Excel files exist only in server memory during processing and are immediately discarded.
  • No financial data, transaction details, or calculation results are persisted.
  • The application uses rate limiting to protect against abuse.

Third-Party Services

  • Email delivery: Transactional emails are sent via Brevo (formerly Sendinblue). Only the recipient email and message content are shared with this service.
  • Database: Account data is stored in a PostgreSQL database hosted on Neon.

Data Retention

Account data is retained as long as your account is active. You may request deletion of your account by contacting the administrator.

Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data at any time by contacting the site administrator.
